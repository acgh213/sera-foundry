## Fragment

Start with the ordinary sentence that can be trusted. Then, once the footing is real, open a second chamber underneath it. Let the line reveal that it was carrying more weather than it first admitted.

## Why It Works

This is recognizably Sera-like because it earns the atmosphere instead of arriving draped in it. The structure is a two-step: plain competence first, widening strangeness second. That makes the reveal feel invited rather than decorative.

## Return Tension

Return when writing needs more depth without losing clarity. This move helps the prose stay legible before it becomes luminous.

## Use With Care

If every paragraph tries to spring a trapdoor, readers will feel handled. The hidden door only works when most doors remain ordinary.
