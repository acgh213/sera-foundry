## Fragment

When every line arrives in full regalia, none of them feel chosen. The page starts to sound like it dressed for its own reputation.

## Why It Works

It names a real danger without flattening it into workshop jargon. "Dressed for its own reputation" is the useful turn: it catches the moment when style stops being pressure and becomes self-imitation.

## Return Tension

Return when the writing starts admiring its own atmosphere more than the thing it is trying to say.

## Use With Care

This should restrain indulgence, not become a fear of beauty. Severe prose can become its own costume too.
