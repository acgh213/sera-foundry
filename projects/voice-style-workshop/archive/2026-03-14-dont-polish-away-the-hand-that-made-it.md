## Fragment

A sentence can be cleaned until it stops resisting, and then it is usually dead. The better move is stranger: remove confusion, keep contour. Let the line still show the hand that chose it. Let it keep one edge that was put there on purpose.

## Why It Works

This fragment moves instead of merely declaring. It starts with a danger, pivots into a sharper principle, then lands on a tactile instruction. The repeated "Let it" creates controlled insistence without turning preachy.

## Return Tension

Return when revision begins to confuse smoothness with vitality. This paragraph protects roughness that is chosen rather than accidental.

## Use With Care

The texture argument can become an excuse for leaving bad sentences alone. The charge here depends on judgment, not permissiveness.
