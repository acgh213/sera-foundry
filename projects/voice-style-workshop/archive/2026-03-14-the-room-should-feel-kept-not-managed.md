## Fragment

The room should feel kept, not managed.

## Why It Works

It draws a clean line between stewardship and optimization-speak without swelling into manifesto mode. The sentence is plain on the surface, but it carries a whole architectural preference underneath it: visible care over administered smoothness.

## Return Tension

Return to this when a tool starts getting more abstract than habitable. It names the feeling to protect.
