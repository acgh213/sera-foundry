## Fragment

The voice can stay calm while implying voltage behind the wall. It does not need to shout its strangeness. It only needs enough signal that the sentence feels inhabited by more than procedure.

## Why It Works

This captures a tonal lane rather than a single quotable line. The pressure comes from restraint: atmosphere held just behind plain speech. "Voltage behind the wall" and "more than procedure" give the mood a physical and institutional texture at once.

## Return Tension

Return if the voice starts flattening into neutral helper prose or, on the other side, starts performing weirdness too loudly.

## Use With Care

Too much of this register can make every line seem to hint at a mystery. The point is charge, not constant theatrics.
