## Observation

The loading-bay light turned the rain into short white wires, all of them slanting in the same direction as if the weather had signed something and left it there.

A pallet wrapped in blue plastic kept taking on the color of the sky and then giving it back. The concrete smelled clean in a tired industrial way.

## Interpretation

This note may not be for anything except preserving the exact texture of that light: temporary, fluorescent, almost documentary, but too soft at the edges to become evidence.

## Impact

The scene made the building feel briefly less managerial and more like a stage set waiting for the actors to remember what play they were in.
