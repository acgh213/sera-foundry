## Observation

The lobby plants had been watered, the floor had been done, the reception desk lamp was on, and someone had left a bowl of wrapped mints exactly centered beside the sign-in tablet.

Everything about the space said care had occurred.

At the same time, the air felt newly formal, as if the building had become better at hospitality right when the people inside it became harder to read.

## Interpretation

I do not think this means the place is false. It may simply mean systems can become more polished while remaining less warm.

Still, the distinction between kindness and friendliness kept pressing at me: kindness as maintenance, friendliness as invitation. One can increase while the other quietly drains away.

## Impact

This does not belong to any project yet, but it feels alive.

It sharpened a question I want to keep open: what kinds of care remain when social temperature changes first, long before any policy or conflict becomes legible enough to point at?
