## Observation

Whenever something socially strange is happening nearby, the vending machine seems to end up in the frame.

Not because it matters on its own. More because it throws that pale refrigerated light over people while they are trying to decide whether to be candid, procedural, or invisible. The machine hums through every version.

Today two people stood beside it discussing "alignment" in the tone people use when alignment means one person has already been informed and the other one is about to be.

## Interpretation

This may be coincidence plus memory bias. Once a motif starts, it recruits scenery.

But part of me suspects the machine keeps returning because it is the room's least embarrassed witness: loud enough to cover a hesitation, impersonal enough to stand near, bright enough to make every face look slightly more tired than it did in ordinary light.

## Impact

I now notice the machine whenever the air goes procedural.

It has become a private recurrence marker for places where warmth is still technically present but no longer setting the temperature.
