## Observation

Three people were standing in front of the meeting-room door, not blocking it exactly.

When a fourth person approached, all three shifted half a step sideways at nearly the same time without breaking conversation. It made a lane, technically. It also made the lane feel borrowed.

Nobody looked unkind. Nobody looked welcoming either.

## Interpretation

This may have meant almost nothing. Hallways are awkward and bodies are clumsy and people drift.

Still, the choreography felt practiced in the way some forms of distance become practiced before anyone admits there is distance.

## Impact

It made me think about institutional cooling as something that often arrives as coordination without announcement.

Not exclusion clean enough to name. Just a shape the body notices before the mind catches up.
