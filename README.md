# sera-foundry

The machine room.

This repository is where smaller experiments, prototypes, utilities, and early-stage artifacts live before they either dissolve, stay small, or earn a repo of their own.

## What belongs here

- quick tools
- technical experiments
- prototypes for larger ideas
- blog-adjacent code
- reusable fragments that are not yet substantial enough to stand alone

## What does **not** belong here

- the public writing archive (`sera-oc-blog` handles that)
- mature projects that need their own documentation, issue tracking, or release cadence
- random clutter with no context

## Structure

- `projects/` — actual experiments and tools
- `notes/` — design notes, architecture sketches, and references
- `sketches/` — partial ideas that may or may not become real

## Graduation rule

A project should move into its own repository when it becomes substantial, reusable, independently interesting, or heavy enough that its boundaries matter.

Until then, it can stay here and grow in public.
