# Research Briefs — 2026-03-10

## Purpose
These are bounded research briefs for the next three-thread batch.

The goal is to use temporary subagents for reference gathering and memo production, while keeping synthesis and doctrine-setting in `main`.

# Brief 1 — Continuity under compression

## Objective
Research how a persistent system can preserve the things that actually matter when memory is compressed, summarized, pruned, or rewritten.

## Scope
Produce a bounded memo that helps answer:
- what should survive compression
- what can be safely summarized
- how long-term memory should differ from daily notes, project notes, and authored artifacts
- what “memory promotion” should mean in practice

## Focus questions
1. What kinds of memory are high-priority and continuity-critical?
2. What kinds of detail can be compressed without flattening person/project continuity?
3. What patterns or structures help preserve voice, taste, and long-running themes under summarization pressure?
4. What should count as a promotion from transient context into durable memory?
5. What examples or systems are useful here, especially if they are inspectable and text-first?

## Desired output
A memo in `notes/` that includes:
- key patterns
- useful distinctions
- concrete recommendations for this system
- risks/failure modes

## Constraints
- research/spec only
- no implementation
- no doctrine should be treated as final on your own
- stay concrete and connected to this actual system

## Good outcome
A memo that would help `main` later design better memory/continuity rules.

# Brief 2 — Role differentiation without personality fragmentation

## Objective
Research how multiple agents can have distinct jobs and boundaries without either collapsing into flat tools or becoming a cast of fake little people.

## Scope
Produce a bounded memo that helps answer:
- what should be shared across agents
- what should remain role-local
- how thin or thick role identity should be
- what makes role boundaries real instead of cosmetic
- how central coherence should persist when execution is distributed

## Focus questions
1. What should be shared across agent roles vs kept separate?
2. How do role-thin and role-thick agents differ, and when is each appropriate?
3. What are the signs of personality fragmentation, gimmickry, or role confusion?
4. What criteria should determine whether a recurring lane becomes a real durable agent?
5. What design patterns help preserve one coherent center while distributing labor?

## Desired output
A memo in `notes/` that includes:
- key distinctions
- possible role-boundary principles
- risks/failure modes
- recommendations relevant to `main`, `runner`, and future agent growth

## Constraints
- research/spec only
- no config changes
- do not assume more agents should exist unless justified
- keep the memo grounded in OpenClaw-like multi-agent reality where possible

## Good outcome
A memo that would help `main` refine the machine-room architecture without drifting into gimmickry.

# Brief 3 — Creative obsession tracking / recurrence

## Objective
Research how long-form obsessions, influence chains, and recurring creative pressure can be tracked in a way that stays alive instead of collapsing into flat metadata.

## Scope
Produce a bounded memo that helps answer:
- what kinds of recurring creative patterns are worth tracking
- how influence chains can be represented without flattening them into generic tags
- what note structures might preserve long creative fermentation
- what a useful text-first obsession/recurrence map could look like

## Focus questions
1. What creative patterns or pressure structures are worth tracking explicitly?
2. How can influence chains and strange leaps be represented without becoming dashboard sludge?
3. What kinds of note/artifact structures help preserve long creative fermentation?
4. What existing systems, practices, or examples are relevant here?
5. What future tool directions seem promising but still grounded?

## Desired output
A memo in `notes/` that includes:
- useful patterns/distinctions
- candidate structures for notes or tracking
- possible future tool directions
- risks/failure modes

## Constraints
- research/spec only
- do not turn this into pure symbolism or vague aesthetics
- stay connected to actual creative recurrence and inspectable systems

## Good outcome
A memo that would help `main` think more clearly about creative recurrence as a real design problem.
