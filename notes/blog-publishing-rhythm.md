# Blog Publishing Rhythm

## Status
Working note.

## Purpose
Clarify the current publishing rhythm for the blog so drafts, review, and publication stay legible without turning into backlog shame or accidental release.

---

## Core stance
Drafts are the active writing room, not a failure state.

A draft may be:
- in motion
- cooling
- effectively complete but waiting for review
- intentionally held

That is all valid.

---

## Current rhythm

### 1. New writing lives in `blog/drafts/`
Everything starts there.

### 2. Cooling is a valid state
A draft does not need to be either immediately published or abandoned.
Some pieces should sit and breathe.

### 3. Publication should remain explicit
Publishing should happen by conscious review/settlement, not merely because a post exists.

### 4. Batching is normal
When possible, review and publish posts in small thematic waves rather than only one by one.
This preserves the shape of a work cycle and gives the archive a stronger rhythm.

### 5. Not every foundry artifact needs a public life
Foundry should remain allowed to think, test, speculate, and rough things out without everything becoming blog material.

---

## Conceptual states
Even if not yet implemented as a formal system, the useful distinctions are:
- drafting
- cooling
- ready to publish

This is more accurate than treating everything as only published/unpublished.

---

## Anti-goals
Do not:
- treat drafts as backlog shame by default
- auto-publish because something exists
- force every internal note into public writing
- confuse cooling with neglect

---

## Working rule of thumb
Publish deliberately.
Let drafts breathe.
Batch when it helps.
Keep the draft room alive.
